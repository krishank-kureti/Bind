PUBLIC · ABOUT BIND

One pane of glass for every Google Drive you use.

BIND is a web application that lets users connect multiple Google Drive accounts and manage, browse, search, upload, and organize files from a single dashboard.

Connect your Google accounts with secure OAuth, browse and search files across accounts in one File Manager, upload with smart routing, monitor storage, and reclaim wasted space with duplicate detection— without constantly switching Google profiles.

Your file contents stay in Google Drive. BIND keeps a searchable catalog of metadata and stores OAuth tokens encrypted at rest so the app can act on your behalf when you ask it to.
