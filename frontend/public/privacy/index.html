Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2026
Service: BIND / CloudVault (“BIND”, “we”, “us”)
Application home page: https://bind-one-zeta.vercel.app/

In plain language BIND helps you manage one or more Google Drive accounts from a single dashboard. Your actual files (photos, docs, videos) stay in Google Drive. We store an account catalog (metadata), encrypted Google login tokens, and app settings so the product can work. We do not host your file contents as a second copy of Drive.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how BIND handles information when you use our website and API at bind-one-zeta.vercel.app (and related backend services). It is written for Google OAuth verification and for users who want a clear picture of data storage.

This document is provided for transparency and product compliance. It is not formal legal advice. If you need a lawyer-reviewed policy for a commercial launch, consult qualified counsel.

2. Information we collect

2.1 From Google when you sign in

When you use “Sign in with Google,” we receive (depending on the scopes you approve):

  • Basic profile data: Google account id, email address, display name, profile photo URL
  • OAuth credentials: access token and, when issued, refresh token, plus expiry timing
  • Drive-related data needed to provide the service after you grant Drive access, including file and folder metadata (names, sizes, MIME types, parents, starred/trash flags, ownership flags, checksums when available, open/view links, thumbnails where provided), and storage quota figures

2.2 Data you generate in BIND

  • App preferences (for example upload routing mode, whether to show shared files, notification preferences)
  • Duplicate-detection groups and related records created by the product
  • Upload job records (filename, size, status, progress, target account)
  • Support-style inputs if you submit forms that only live in the browser (if any are not sent to our servers, they are not stored by us)

2.3 Technical data

  • Session cookie so we know you are logged in
  • Server logs (timestamps, error messages, account ids) used to operate and debug the service
  • Browser local storage for theme and similar UI preferences on your device

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate you and keep you signed in
  • Connect and manage one or more Google Drive accounts under your BIND user
  • Build and refresh a searchable catalog of your Drive items (the “file index”)
  • Perform actions you request (list, search, upload, rename, move, star, trash, copy, duplicate cleanup, etc.)
  • Show storage usage and related dashboard analytics
  • Secure the service, prevent abuse, and fix outages
  • Comply with law where required

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Where data is stored (infrastructure)

Depending on deployment, BIND uses third-party processors:

  • Frontend hosting — typically Vercel (static web app)
  • Backend API — typically Render (Node.js / Express)
  • Primary database — PostgreSQL, typically Neon (users, connected accounts, file index metadata, quotas, settings, duplicate records, upload jobs)
  • Cache & sessions — Redis, typically Upstash (login sessions and short-lived list caches)
  • Google — Google Identity / OAuth and Google Drive remain the systems of record for authentication with Google and for your file contents

Processors may store data in regions they operate (often the United States or multi-region). Review their policies for subprocessors and locations.

5. File contents vs what BIND stores

File binaries BIND is not designed as a second full backup of every Drive file. Your documents, images, and other file bodies remain in Google Drive. When you open a file, BIND typically sends you to Google (for example via a Drive view link). When you upload or move across accounts, content may transit our servers temporarily to complete the request, then live again in Drive.

The file index in our database is a rebuildable catalog (names, hierarchy, sizes, flags, links, checksums when available). It can be refreshed by syncing from Drive.

6. Encryption and access to tokens

Google OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored in our database encrypted at rest using industry-standard symmetric encryption (AES-256-GCM). Ciphertext is stored in a form that is not readable as a plain Google token without the application encryption key.

The encryption key is held as a server secret (environment configuration on the backend host). It is not sent to your browser. Ordinary API responses to the frontend do not include your raw or decrypted Google tokens.

Important honesty note: Encryption protects tokens stored in the database from casual inspection. Anyone who can control both the production database and the server encryption secret could, in principle, decrypt tokens (as with any server-side encryption system). BIND is designed so that day-to-day product surfaces and operators do not need to view tokens in plaintext, and so that file content remains with Google Drive rather than being archived by us. We do not claim that no privileged operator could ever access systems that hold keys—we claim secure storage practices and least exposure.

Session identifiers are stored in browser cookies (httpOnly) and session data in Redis, separate from Google token ciphertext.

7. Google API / limited use

BIND’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, when those policies apply.

Drive access is used only to provide user-facing cloud storage management features you request (browse, search, organize, upload, analytics, duplicate detection, multi-account tools), not to sell personal data or use Drive content for unrelated advertising.

8. Sharing of information

We share data only as needed to run the service, for example:

  • With infrastructure providers listed above (hosting, database, cache)
  • With Google, when you connect accounts or we call Drive APIs on your behalf
  • If required by law, valid legal process, or to protect rights and safety
  • With your direction (for example actions you trigger in the product)

9. Retention

  • Account and index data are kept while your BIND account and connected Drive accounts remain linked
  • Disconnecting a Drive account removes that connection and associated indexed metadata for that account (subject to backups and logs aging out)
  • Sessions expire according to session configuration (on the order of a day unless renewed by use)
  • Short-lived caches expire within seconds to minutes
  • Server logs are retained only as long as needed for operations and security

10. Your choices

  • Disconnect Google accounts from BIND
  • Revoke BIND’s access in your Google Account permissions
  • Clear browser local data for theme/preferences
  • Stop using the service
  • Contact us to request deletion of BIND account data where feasible

11. Security

We use HTTPS in production, encrypted token storage, session cookies with security flags appropriate to production, and access controls on infrastructure accounts. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

12. Children

BIND is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. International users

If you access BIND from outside the region where our processors operate, your information may be processed in other countries with different data-protection rules.
